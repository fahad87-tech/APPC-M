3. Qualitative Physics Pre-Check (Section 1 Solution)

Free-Body Diagram at Loop Apex: Exactly two downward vectors directed toward loop centre: Gravitational force Fg = mg (downward) and Track Normal Force FN (downward). Common Student Error: Drawing an outward "centrifugal force" or an upward normal force. Zero credit for non-existent forces.

Rehearsal Drop Physical Explanation: At release height h, the ball possesses gravitational potential energy Ugi = mgh. At the apex (y = 2r), required centripetal acceleration is ac = vapex²/r. If vapex² ≥ gr, normal force FN ≥ 0, cleanly maintaining track contact.

Empirical Best-Fit Linear Equation (5 Points):

• Slope (a): 14.000 m/s²  |  • Y-Intercept (b): -0.000 m²/s²
Y-Intercept Interpretation: The small negative intercept (b = -0.000 m²/s²) physically reflects loop entry friction and rolling resistance threshold required before net horizontal exit velocity emerges.

Energy Check & Dissipation Fee (Section 4 Analysis):
• Ideal theoretical point-mass slope: 2g = 2(9.80) = 19.60 m/s² (100% translational mechanical efficiency).
• Measured apparatus slope: a = 14.000 m/s².
• Translational Energy Retained: η = (14.000 / 19.60) × 100% = 71.4%.
• Mechanical Deficit / Dissipation Fee: 100% − 71.4% = 28.6%.
Physical Explanation: ≈ 28.6% of initial gravitational potential energy is converted into rotational kinetic energy of the rolling solid sphere (I = ⅖mr²), rolling friction against rails, and micro-vibrations.

5. Target Range Inversion & Theoretical Formula Contrast

To demonstrate why empirical calibration is required, two test ranges below the maximum apparatus range (Xmax = 113.5 cm) are solved below:

🎯 SAMPLE TARGET A: Contracted Landing Range X* = 95.0 cm (0.950 m)
1. Required Exit Speed Squared:
(v*)² = g(X*)² / (2H) = 9.80(0.950)² / [2(0.773)] = 5.7209 m²/s²  (v* = 2.392 m/s).
2. Empirical Calibration Inversion (True Setting):

h* = [(v*)² − b] / a = [5.7209 − (-0.0000)] / 14.0000 = 40.86 cm (0.4086 m)
3. Theoretical Formula Comparison (What naive theory predicts):
• Ideal Sliding Point-Mass (mgh = ½mv²): hideal* = (v*)² / (2g) = 5.7209 / 19.60 = 29.19 cm (0.2919 m).
• Pure Rolling Solid Sphere (mgh = ⁷⁄₁₀mv²): hrolling* = 7(v*)² / (10g) = 40.86 cm (0.4086 m).
DISCREPANCY ALERT: Ideal sliding theory underpredicts required height by 28.6% (pure rolling underpredicts by 0.0%)! Releasing at 29.2 cm is below 2.5r (23.0 cm) and would fall off at the loop apex!

🎯 SAMPLE TARGET B: Contracted Landing Range X* = 105.0 cm (1.050 m)
1. Required Exit Speed Squared:
(v*)² = g(X*)² / (2H) = 9.80(1.050)² / [2(0.773)] = 6.9887 m²/s²  (v* = 2.644 m/s).
2. Empirical Calibration Inversion (True Setting):

h* = [(v*)² − b] / a = [6.9887 − (-0.0000)] / 14.0000 = 49.92 cm (0.4992 m)
• Safe Limit Verification: h* = 49.92 cm < hmax = 58.3 cm and ≥ 2.5r (23.0 cm) (🟢 SAFE & VALID ON APPARATUS).
3. Theoretical Formula Underprediction: Ideal sliding hideal* = 35.66 cm (28.6% deficit) | Pure rolling hrolling* = 49.92 cm (0.0% deficit).

6. Student Exit Reflection Solutions & Official Scoring Rubric

Q1. Why use the calibrated line (v² = ah + b) rather than ideal physics?
Acceptable Answer: Real apparatus experiences mechanical energy loss to rotational inertia (I = ⅖mr²) and track friction. The empirical fit accounts directly for these real-world losses without arbitrary fudge factors.

Q2. What does the 28.6% slope deficit prove about mechanical energy?
Acceptable Answer: The slope deficit proves translational kinetic energy at exit is ~28.6% less than gravitational potential energy at release, proving non-conservative energy transfer away from linear motion.
