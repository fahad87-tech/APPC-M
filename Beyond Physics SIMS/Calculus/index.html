Viewing the laboratory table from directly above. The ruler is mounted vertically across the table. When stretched, the ruler bends to the RIGHT, storing elastic strain energy $PE = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$. Upon release, the ruler snaps back toward the left, strikes the ball at the neutral point, propelling it horizontally to the LEFT along the graduated centimeter track. The stopping distance $d = \frac{v^2}{2\mu_k g} \propto A^2$ proves that doubling the bend launches the marble 4 times farther!
